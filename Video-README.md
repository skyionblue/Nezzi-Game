# That Golf Cartoon

## Overview

**That Golf Cartoon** is a comedic 2D anime-style cartoon concept set in a world where traditional professional golfers have gone on strike because tournament payouts have dropped dramatically. While the sport is left without its stars, an unexpected replacement emerges: massive bodybuilders who completely reinvent golf.

Instead of clubs, they wield giant sledgehammers.

Instead of golf balls, they use bowling balls.

The result is an over-the-top action-comedy that transforms golf into a ridiculous spectacle full of anime energy, exaggerated physics, and hilarious visual gags.

---

# Storyboard Idea 1 – The Strike (Recommended)

## Scene 1 (0–3 seconds)

An empty professional golf course.

A group of frustrated, average golfers wearing colorful polo shirts stand outside the clubhouse holding signs that read:

- "Fair Pay!"
- "Golfers on Strike!"

TV reporters and cameras capture the protest while the golfers look disappointed.

---

## Scene 2 (3–7 seconds)

The ground begins to shake.

A squad of gigantic, muscular bodybuilders wearing sleeveless golf polos, tiny visors, golf gloves, and golf shoes march onto the course carrying enormous steel sledgehammers over their shoulders.

The entrance is dramatic with:

- anime speed lines
- dust clouds
- heroic poses
- slow-motion camera movement

---

## Scene 3 (7–12 seconds)

Instead of placing golf balls on tees, they carefully place enormous black bowling balls.

One bodybuilder takes an exaggerated anime stance before swinging a giant chrome sledgehammer.

The bowling ball launches across the course like a missile with:

- shockwaves
- glowing speed trails
- flying dirt
- exaggerated impact effects

---

## Scene 4 (12–15 seconds)

The bowling ball crashes into an oversized golf hole with an earth-shaking explosion.

The bodybuilders celebrate by:

- flexing
- cheering
- posing

Traditional golfers watch from behind a fence in complete disbelief.

Fade to the title:

# THAT GOLF CARTOON

# Storyboard Idea 3 – Sports News

## 0–5 seconds

An anime-style news anchor announces:

> "Breaking News! Golfers are on strike!"

Cut to empty PGA-style fairways.

---

## 5–10 seconds

The reporter turns as giant bodybuilders march past carrying sledgehammers and bowling balls.

---

## 10–15 seconds

One perfect swing launches the bowling ball through sand traps and across the course before it crashes into the cup with a massive anime explosion.

Freeze frame on everyone celebrating.

Title card:

# THAT GOLF CARTOON

# Recommendation

**Storyboard 1** is the strongest option for a 15-second trailer.

It clearly establishes:

1. The problem (golfers on strike)
2. The unexpected replacement (bodybuilders)
3. The absurd new rules (sledgehammers and bowling balls)
4. A memorable comedic ending

This structure provides the clearest narrative while maximizing visual humor and anime-style action within a short runtime.
